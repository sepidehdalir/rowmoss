How do repeats work?

The total is completed rows. At zero, your next row is1. Finish a row, then tap. At a repeat boundary, the next position returns to row1 of the next repeat.

How do I undo a reset?

Tap Undo. It also works after reopening the app. Up to100 earlier actions are kept per project.

The app offers a recovered position

Check its saved total and repeat position before accepting it. A previous file can be one action behind. If neither file opens, keep the app installed and contact support. Do not reinstall as a troubleshooting step; deleting the app removes local data.

Where are my projects?

The work basket shows saved projects. One project is free; Lifetime Pro adds more projects, reusable repeats and history. Existing projects stay accessible after entitlement changes. There is no cloud sync.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases in Settings or Lifetime Pro with the Apple Account used to buy it. Apple handles billing. If restore fails, check connectivity and the account, then retry.
