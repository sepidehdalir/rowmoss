Rowmoss Privacy

Effective October 17, 2026

Rowmoss is a local knitting and crochet row counter by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir / Arvinify.

Project names, repeat patterns, row counts, undo and count history stay on your device. We do not operate a server for your projects, create accounts, provide cloud sync, collect analytics, display ads or use tracking SDKs.

Apple processes in-app purchases. Rowmoss reads verified StoreKit entitlements to enable Lifetime Pro. We do not receive your payment information. Local diagnostic events contain no project content and are not sent to us.

Deleting Rowmoss removes its local library. Device backups may include its app data according to your Apple backup settings. Rowmoss does not promise cross-device sync or an independent cloud backup.

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Please avoid sending sensitive information. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo-library access.

Questions: hello@arvinify.com. Support.
